Privacy Policy

Thomas Rigby Personal Tools · Last updated November 4, 2026

Thomas Rigby Personal Tools are personal scripts and apps used only by their author. They are not offered to anyone else.

What they access

Depending on the tool, and only with the user's permission, these tools may use Google APIs to read, send, and organize the user's Gmail; read and manage Google Calendar events; read Google Contacts; and read, create, and edit files in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides.

Where data goes

Google data is processed on the user's own computer. Some tools may keep a local copy of the data they need. There is no server, and Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.

When the user asks an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) to help with a task, the Google data needed for that task is sent to Anthropic to produce the response. This only happens at the user's request.

Google API Services

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tools' local folders removes every local copy.
