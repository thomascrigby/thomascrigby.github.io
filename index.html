Thomas Rigby Personal Tools

Personal scripts and apps for one Google account

Thomas Rigby Personal Tools is a set of small scripts and apps that Thomas Rigby built for his own use on his own Mac. Depending on the tool, they may work with his Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides through Google's official APIs.

They aren't offered to the public. Nobody else signs in to them.
